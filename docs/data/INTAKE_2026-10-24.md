# Data intake, 24 Oct 2026: the owner's Kaggle sources

The owner supplied Kaggle datasets to fill the data holes (P0.6 of [ML_PHASE0.md](../ML_PHASE0.md)). Nothing here is committed or
published: the files were downloaded to a scratch folder, profiled, and compared with sources we already hold. **Every dataset below is
`external_unverified` until it is imported through the validation in [EXTERNAL_DATA.md](../EXTERNAL_DATA.md); none of it may be used as
evidence to qualify a strategy, make paper-gate progress or promote a model.** Licence: **not yet known** for any of them (a download
carries no licence file; the owner reads it off the Kaggle page). Until it is known, nothing is placed in a public release.

- **Timestamps** use the desk's convention (start of the minute, 09:15 to 15:29). A shift test against aeron7 confirmed no one-minute offset
  (median close difference 0.000 unshifted, 1.95 points (NIFTY) and 6.4 points (BANKNIFTY) when shifted by a minute).
- **The daily close differs from Yahoo's by about 0.05%** because the last minute's close is not NSE's official close (the index series prints one
  flat price from 15:15 to about 15:28, see MASTER_PLAN section 4). Never build a daily close from the last minute: use the official close
  (the same rule the v4 sleeves follow).
- **The 6% one-minute move** is one session in each index. *Correction, 24 Oct (after the importer ran):* it is **13 March 2020**, not the 4 June 2024 election
  result guessed here: a long flat stretch, then +6.0% (NIFTY) and +5.9% (BANKNIFTY) in the same minute. A real event must not be quarantined, so the importer
  sends such sessions to a review queue (excluded by default, admitted only with evidence) instead of dropping them: see EXTERNAL_DATA.md.

### Cross-source test against aeron7 (2015-01-09 to 2023-02-28: 738,385 NIFTY and 738,112 BANKNIFTY common minutes)

| | NIFTY | BANKNIFTY |
|---|---|---|
| Close identical, minute by minute | 84.9% | 89.1% |
| Close within 1.1 points (99th percentile) | yes | 3.1 points |
| Minutes with a close difference above 5 points | 818 (46 sessions) | 4,266 (628 sessions) |
| **Realised variance per session, ratio Kaggle / aeron7 (median)** | **1.000** | **1.000** |
| Sessions with the ratio within plus or minus 10% | **99.8%** | **99.7%** |
| Correlation of log realised variance | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| Per-year 5th to 95th percentile of the ratio | 0.97 to 1.03 (2019); 0.99 to 1.01 (other years) | 0.99 to 1.01 |

**Reading:** for returns and realised variance the two sources agree almost exactly, in every year 2015 to 2022. They differ more in highs and lows
(about 12 to 15% of minutes differ in open, high or low), so **range-based estimators must not be mixed across the two sources**. The two may
share an upstream, so agreement is not proof of accuracy against NSE; the agreement with Yahoo's daily bars and the desk's own convention are the
independent anchors.

### The two BANKNIFTY datasets are the same data

`sandeepkapri/banknifty-data-upto-2024` against the BANKNIFTY file of `debashis74017/nifty-50-minute-data`: 1,042,806 common minutes, **100.000% identical OHLC** (one minute differs, in 2024, by 2.75 points in the low and 7.55 in the close). The second ends earlier (22 Apr 2026, against 15 May 2026) and
has 103 minutes the first lacks. So they share an upstream and agreement between them says nothing about accuracy. The independent anchors for
BANKNIFTY are the aeron7 comparison above (to Feb 2023) and Yahoo's daily bars.

## 3. Decisions (provisional, until the importer passes)

1. **Index minutes, Mar 2023 to 14 May 2026, NIFTY and BANKNIFTY:** take from the first dataset (`T1b`). Use close-to-close returns and realised variance;
   do not build range-based features across the aeron7 boundary (end Feb 2023).
2. **15 May 2026** is dropped (truncated session). Short sessions (under 360 minutes) and truncated ones are **left out of the imported partitions** and listed with
   their reason in the manifest and quality report; they are not kept in the data (EXTERNAL_DATA.md).
3. **INDIA VIX minutes:** import with the 2,547 invalid rows (2018 to 2019) **quarantined**, not repaired; flag the series as noisy; it must not feed anything
   before the Kotak INDIA VIX payload question (audit N1) is settled.
4. **Option chain:** exploratory features only. IV, delta, gamma, theta, vega and rho are **not used**: the author's IV is non-positive or above 500
   in 4,264,804 of 35,991,045 rows (11.8%), and nothing says how it was computed. `ltp` is a traded price, not a quote; there is no bid or ask.
   A day with only 747 rows (against a median of 108,278) is a partial day to be listed and excluded.
5. **Storage:** none of this goes into the public repository or a public release until the licence is known and allows redistribution. Derived
   tables (our own parquet, with a manifest and sha256) are the only thing that may be published, and only then.
