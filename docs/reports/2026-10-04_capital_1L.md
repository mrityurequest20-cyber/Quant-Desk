# Report card: the desk at ₹1,00,000 (4 Oct 2026)

The intraday paper account moved from ₹20,000 to **₹1,00,000** (commit 3b34fbe; the desk resets to it on its next run,
having no trades yet). Then every analysis the system has was run on all the data it holds. Each result is labelled by
what it is evidence of. Labels are never mixed.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **REAL-PIT** | Real option quotes at the moment of decision: the only evidence that can qualify a strategy |
| **REAL-EOD** | Real option prices, but end-of-day (bhavcopy open → close): not point in time |
| **REAL-UNDERLYING** | Real index or stock prices; no option prices involved |
| **MODELLED** | Option prices from the desk's model (bhavcopy IV / India VIX): scenario analysis only |

## Verdict in one line

**No proven edge yet, at ₹20k or ₹1L.** Real data says buying index options open → close has lost. The one validated
improvement is a better move-size forecast. Risk control worked: the worst simulated drawdown was capped at about 15%,
against −38% for buy-and-hold NIFTY. The only real-quote evidence is 1 session, and 28 are needed.

## 3. Options in the model world at ₹1L (MODELLED, scenario analysis only)

53 sessions (20 Jul → 1 Oct 2026), 100,320 plan outcomes. The calibrated-logit baseline was replayed through the live
engine's own risk rules with **₹1,00,000**: one position at a time, 2 trades a day, cooldowns, square-off.
- **At ₹1L almost nothing is unaffordable any more.** At ₹20k most 3–5 and 6+ DTE plans were.
- **20 configurations (horizon × DTE): none passed** the development gates.
  - The model abstains on 89–100% of decision points: it trades only 0–24 times in 53 sessions.
  - Net ranged from −₹31,856 (close, 1 DTE) to +₹32,543 (60m, 0 DTE: 6 trades, 90% interval −1.0 R … +2.9 R).
  - Every positive interval spans zero. Three negative ones don't.
- Read as: too few trades to tell anything, in a world whose option prices are modelled.

## What ₹1,00,000 changes and what it doesn't

**Changes:**
- Plans that were unaffordable now fit.
- BANKNIFTY becomes tradable.
- One round trip's costs fall from 0.6% to 0.12% of the account.

**Doesn't change:** expectancy per trade, which is still negative in real EOD data. Capital doesn't create edge; it
only changes how much each trade is worth.

**Owner's call:** risk per trade is still 8% of equity (₹8,000 at ₹1L). That figure was forced by the ₹20k account. At
₹1L a single NIFTY lot is 1.2–1.6% of equity, so **2–3%** would do. Tightening it is safer; nothing was changed
without you.
