## 7. Things learned the hard way
- A classifier denial is the owner's decision: do not work around it (history/force-push, ruleset edits via API, self-modifying AUTONOMY.md before an explicit grant were all denied; the owner did them by hand).
- Squash merges of stacked PRs conflict: after merging a base PR, merge `origin/main` into the stacked branch and keep the branch's side for files that are a superset (verify `git diff origin/main <old-head>` is empty first).
- `gh release view` (GraphQL) is blocked in the sandbox; `gh api` works. `ReleaseStore --pull` works in Actions.
- Idle subagents that "wait on background work" re-report in a loop and burn usage; stop them with TaskStop after reading the report.
- Replayed setups from underlying-only data are NOT a faithful stand-in for the desk (the EV floor rejects almost everything); never train ML on them as if they were desk decisions.
